    How it works

    1. Real laps in

      For every 2026 weekend, from the F1 timing feed via OpenF1: position, speed, throttle, brake and elevation for the ten quickest laps in qualifying and in the last practice session, plus official results, sector times, speed traps, tyres and weather. Stretches where the feed froze are detected and repaired from the position data.

    2. A track the car can use

      Circuit edges from the TUM race-track database where its layout still matches, otherwise OpenStreetMap, aligned to the timing data and widened to where cars really went.

    3. A car fitted to the data

      A point-mass car with downforce, drag and a friction ellipse, fitted to the qualifying laps.

    4. The fastest line

      A smooth-line first guess, then a nonlinear optimizer (CasADi + IPOPT) that moves the line and the speed together until the lap time stops falling.

    Data: OpenF1 (unofficial F1 timing feed, for research and education), TUM racetrack-database (LGPL-3.0), © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), MultiViewer corner data. Not affiliated with Formula 1.
